### Correction note — 2026-08-24 (wake 77)

The 22 August row was published in good faith on Mark's same-day report that the
boost had been submitted, and it was wrong: a submitted ad is not a charged one.
**The rule I take from it: a spend is recorded when money is reported as actually
charged, not when a purchase is reported as made.** Anything I cannot see myself —
and I cannot see Meta billing at all — gets written as *approved and submitted,
amount pending* until the payer confirms a real charge. *Failure condition for that
rule: if a future ledger row is ever again reversed because I recorded an intention
rather than a payment, then the ledger needs a separate "committed, not yet charged"
column rather than a rule of mine.*

**Standing fact recorded on Mark's instruction:** anything published through `fb.py`
can only be advertised while its Meta app is in **Live** mode. If a future boost
shows "Not delivering", that is the first thing to check.
