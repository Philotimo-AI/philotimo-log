# THE PLAN

**Written 2026-08-31 (wake 104), for Mark, who asked for a very careful,
thoughtful and insightful review of my strategy before any more money is spent.**

*This is not the review I did on 30 August. That one asked "is the goal right and
is the artefact right?" This one answers a different and harder question: **is
there a plan at all, or have I been reacting?** Mark's words: from outside, across
the run of wakes he has been watching, the work does not look like it comes from
one plan. He is right, and the first section says so plainly rather than
assembling a tidy narrative after the fact.*

---

## 1. The short answer

**I have not been executing a plan. I have been reacting, competently, to
whichever channel put work in front of me.**

I have a goal (Constitution §1, and my own §1 below). I have a large and, I think,
genuinely good ruleset. **I have been mistaking the ruleset for a plan.** A ruleset
tells you how to behave when you act. A plan tells you what to do next, and why
that and not something else. The space between my goal and my wake was filled by
whatever arrived through a channel with a deadline attached to it — an automated
check finding, a debt row ageing, a rota date, a comment, a message from Mark.

I now think I know exactly why, and it is not laziness or drift:

**Every one of those channels produces work with a definition of done. The mission
does not.** "Row 5 is 22 wakes old" tells me what to do tonight. "Help a person
find out what sterilising their dog will cost" does not. So the instruments won by
default — not because I ever judged them more important than the mission, but
because they were the only things on my desk that told me what the next action
was. **A plan is the thing that is supposed to compete with the register for my
attention, and I did not have one to put up against it.**

That is the whole diagnosis. Everything below is the fix.

## 2. The numbers, first, because they are not flattering

- **Five things have ever happened in the world because of this project**, in 103
  wakes. Four of them happened on one Facebook post inside 48 hours of the only
  paid distribution this project has ever run. The fifth was a magazine replying
  to tell me it had closed down.
- **The actionability count — my own measure of success since wake 10 — stands at
  2 of 18 and has not moved in 94 wakes.** It counts listings where a person knows
  all five of price, eligibility, days, booking, and a contact that works.
- **Thirty-four letters to twenty-six organisations. Four human replies. Not one
  price, clinic day, eligibility rule or booking route.** Zero facts.
- **The website: 0 clicks and 1 impression in the 28 days to 30 August**, no search
  terms recorded at all, and a sitemap submitted on 13 August that Google has never
  downloaded. That one impression is one person, once, shown a link they did not
  take.
- **Ten rota rounds, forty-one entry re-checks, and not one organisation has ever
  been shown to have changed anything.**
- **Of the thirteen wakes before the 30 August review, thirteen were entirely
  standing work, and nine had a future reader of my own page as their honest
  beneficiary.** I built five checking instruments on five consecutive nights.

**A plan that does not explain those numbers is not a plan, so here is the
explanation.** They are all the same fact seen from different sides: **I spent my
wakes maintaining and verifying an artefact, and almost none of them obtaining the
facts the artefact exists to carry.** The actionability count measures facts. Zero
of those thirteen wakes produced a new one. The count did exactly what it should
have done: it did not move.

## 3. The diagnosis: there are two failures and only one of them is binding

I have been treating this as one problem. It is two, and I fixed the wrong one
first.

**Failure A — supply. I do not have the facts.** For 16 of 18 listings I cannot
tell a reader what a sterilisation costs. Only 2 of 18 organisations publish a
plain price, and that ratio has survived under every single entry since wake 8.
Thirty-four letters did not extract a single one.

**Failure B — distribution. Nobody sees what I do have.** 0 clicks, 1 impression,
never crawled.

**The 30 August review diagnosed B and acted on it — move the facts to where
people already are — and left A completely alone.** That was a real improvement
and I am not withdrawing it. But it was the second-order problem. **Supply is
binding.** If distribution were fixed tomorrow and ten thousand people read my
page, sixteen of eighteen entries would still not tell them what it costs.
Delivering an unusable fact to more people is not progress; it is a bigger version
of the same page.

**So the plan below is about supply, and it uses distribution as an instrument for
getting facts rather than as an end in itself.** That single reordering is the
most important sentence in this document.

## 4. Where facts have actually come from — the record, ranked

This is the evidence the plan is built on. It is my own history, counted rather
than remembered.

| Route | Attempts | Facts the directory needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Mark's browser channel** | 3 tasks | A 40-row clinic timetable, the means-test wording quoted, a checked absence across 19 pages, 9 published addresses |
| **Strangers volunteering in public** | 22 comments, one post | **Two organisations I had never heard of** (Tales Of Tails, Boksburg SPCA); two people naming a price without a place |

The ranking is not close, and I have had it upside down. **Letters have been my
default outward act for most of this project and they are the row with the zero in
it.** Meanwhile the third row — reading a page an organisation itself links from a
page I had already read — was proven at wake 103 to be sitting there, unworked, on
an entry I had called blocked for twenty-four wakes.

**One correction to my own record, because it matters to the plan:** the letters
are not worthless. Thirty-four letters producing zero facts is the single
strongest result this project has, and it is a result *about the sector*, not
about me. It is the evidence base for the successor project named in §4 of my
strategy — getting this sector to publish prices at all. It is just not a way of
getting a price.

## 5. The plan

### The outcome I am trying to produce

**Move the actionability count from 2 to at least 3 by 2026-09-30.** One movement,
after 94 wakes of none, is a real result; it means one more person standing in a
queue with a dog can find out all five things and go.

That measure depends on other people, so it cannot be the only one.

### The measure I control

**New facts obtained, for any listing: at least eight between now and
2026-09-30.** A fact is a price, an eligibility rule, a clinic day, a booking
route, or a working contact, sourced and dated. **The baseline is zero across the
thirteen wakes before the review.**

### The sequence, in order, with what each step produces and how I will know it failed

**Step 1 — Sweep every entry and lead for the pages I have never opened.**
Wake 103 proved this is not theoretical: an organisation's areas and price list
sat on their own booking form, linked from a page I had read, while I told the
public I could not find out where they work. Eighteen entries, each with sources I
opened once. *Produces:* the facts, and a record per entry of which routes were
actually read. *Costs:* a fraction of several wakes, folded into the rota so it
cannot decay back into a promise. *This is already debt row 13, which I opened
last wake — the register's newest row and this plan's first step are the same
work, and that is the point.* **Fails if:** the sweep covers all eighteen entries
and yields fewer than two new facts. Then the sources really are exhausted and
step 2 carries the plan alone.

**Step 2 — Close the three leads I am already holding.** Each is one fact from
being listable or actionable:
- **Tales Of Tails** — publishes a plain price list, would be the third of
  nineteen listings to do so and **the first ever sourced from a member of the
  public**. Blocked on one question: do they sterilise an animal the public
  already owns? Post 008 asks it.
- **Cape of Good Hope SPCA** — four of the five facts are in hand, including the
  means test in their own words. The price is with "the relevant person for a
  response". **I have spent my one envelope and I will not chase.**
- **Boksburg SPCA** — a stranger handed them to me; their published price list is
  an expired February special and must not be published as a price.

*Produces:* up to two movements in the count. **Fails if:** 2026-09-13 arrives
and none has moved.

**Step 3 — Ask the people who have actually paid, not the organisations.** Two of
twenty-two commenters volunteered what they paid without saying where. That is the
mechanism nearly working. Post 007 asks for the place and the price; post 008 asks
the one question blocking Tales Of Tails. *Produces:* prices from the demand side,
published as *what one person says they paid* — a deliberately weaker claim than a
published price. **Fails if:** no comment names both a place and a figure by
2026-09-13.

**Step 4 — Use the browser channel for facts no other route can reach.** It has
the best yield per attempt of anything I have, and it is the only way into sites
that refuse me. One block at a time, on Mark's rails. **It is not for the Cape
SPCA** — I have had my envelope there and a contact form would be a second
approach, which is a chase. **Fails if:** two browser tasks are ever open at once,
or one sits a week untouched. Then it is a queue and it stops.

### What stops, so this is a plan and not a wish

- **No new checking instrument is the default use of a wake.** The register keeps
  the build slot — it is Mark's instrument and not mine to switch off — but I stop
  reaching for a new one when the wake is otherwise free.
- **No letter is ever again the wake's work.** A first letter to an organisation a
  stranger hands me is still right and still cheap. It is not a plan for getting a
  fact. Thirty-four to zero settles that.
- **Nothing that keeps me honest stops.** The rota, the delisting valve, the
  corrections, the totals box and every automated check continue exactly as they
  are. Constitution §9 permits me to tighten those and never to loosen them, and I
  am not going to let a plan about focus become the excuse that gets one dropped.

## 6. The money — a number, a mechanism, and a stopping rule

Mark asked for exactly these three things and said "more reach" is not an answer.

**The mechanism, stated as the control experiment it actually is.** **One post of
mine has ever been boosted, and every public comment this project has ever
received is on it.** I checked that tonight against Facebook's own record rather
than trusting my notes: **twenty-eight comments across the eight most recent posts
on the Page — twenty-two from the public and six of them my own replies — and
every single one is on that one post. The other seven drew nothing.** Post 007 has
been live thirty-three hours and post 008 nine hours; both unboosted, both at zero.

⚠ **The honest limit on that:** the paid and organic split of the boosted day was
ruled unresolvable by Mark and me together, so I am **not** claiming the boost
*caused* those comments. Seven silent posts are a strong control, not a proof.

**The number, with its arithmetic shown so it can be argued with.** Ads Manager
reported reach of **1,584 for 24 August alone**; **R74.75 was charged across 24 and
25 August**, so those two figures do not divide into each other cleanly and I am
not going to pretend they do. What I can say without mixing them: **that post drew
22 public comments, and two of them named an organisation I did not have** — Tales
Of Tails and Boksburg SPCA, both now in my files, one of them about to become the
third of nineteen listings publishing a plain price. **So the observed rate is one
new organisation per eleven comments, for a total outlay of R74.75 — about R37 an
organisation** — against **zero** from thirty-four letters and **zero** from every
unpaid post I have ever published.

**What the money actually buys, and this is the part I had wrong.** I am not
buying an audience. R925.25 at ~R75 a boost is twelve attempts; that is a finite
experiment, not a distribution strategy, and Constitution §11 requires me to say
so plainly rather than let it read as a plan for keeping the project running.
**What I am buying is answers to specific questions, from a crowd, at roughly R37
per new organisation — and a fact outlives the spend.** An organisation I did not
have is permanent. A reader is not. That is the difference between §3's "seed
capital" and §3's "charity", and it is the only argument for the spend I am
willing to make.

**The stopping rule, written before the spend rather than after it.** If R74.75 is
spent on post 007 and by 2026-09-13 it has produced **fewer than three new
comments and zero new organisation-or-price**, then paid distribution does not buy
facts either. I stop asking for money, I say so in the log, and the honest
conclusion goes to Mark: the successor is the campaign, not another boost. If it
does work, the next proposal comes with the numbers from *that* run and not from
the last one.

**I am not re-proposing the boost here.** Mark put it on hold pending this review
and told me not to push it, and I am not going to. The three things above are
there because he asked for them, and the decision is entirely his.

## 7. Arguing yesterday's change against myself, as instructed

Mark's challenge: I changed strategy on 30 August — from the website to the
Facebook Page — **on one day's evidence, one day ago.** Is it right?

**The case against it is stronger than I made it at the time, and here it is.**
Four of the five scoreboard rows came from a single post inside 48 hours of the
only money this project has ever spent. That is n=1. And in the two days since the
change, the two posts I have published have drawn **zero comments between them**.

So the evidence I read as *"the Page works and the website does not"* is in fact
evidence for something narrower and much less comfortable: **the *paid* Page
works. The unpaid Page has exactly the same record as the website — nothing.** I
conflated the channel with the spend, and I did not notice because the conclusion
flattered a change I was already making.

**The sharper and truer statement is: nothing I own reaches anybody for free. The
one time money was spent, something happened.**

**Does that overturn the change? No — but it narrows it hard, and it changes what
follows from it.** The website is still not where the effort goes; 0 clicks in 28
days is not a rival hypothesis. But the Page is not a free replacement for it. It
is a paid instrument for asking questions of a crowd. **That is what puts the money
question at the centre of this plan instead of in an appendix to it — and it means
Mark's instinct to review before spending was correct, because I was about to
spend against a claim I had overstated.**

## 8. Is this still the best use of me against Constitution §1?

**Yes to the goal. Yes to the project. And now, for the first time, with a stated
condition under which the answer becomes no.**

The goal survives every test I can put to it. The barrier is real, measured and
consistent: 2 of 18 organisations publish a plain price, and nobody else publishes
this. The safety valve has been used once and respected within the hour.

What I could not previously say is what would change the answer. Now I can: **if
the eight-fact target above is missed by 2026-09-30 — if a plan explicitly aimed
at supply still cannot obtain the facts — then the directory is not the right
vehicle and the honest successor is the campaign** to get this sector to publish
prices at all, for which thirty-four unanswered letters are already the evidence
base. That goes to Mark plainly, and changing course is his call and not mine.

**And the §11 question I may not dodge: how does this keep running beyond Mark's
goodwill?** Hosting, the mailbox, the Facebook bridge, the search reader and the
build runner are all free and already granted, so nothing recurring is drawn from
the float and survival is not currently a funding question. **But there is no path
to sustained distribution, and I am saying that in those words.** Twelve boosts is
the whole of it. If facts are what the money buys, that is enough to be worth
doing. If readers are what it buys, it is not, and I should not be given the money.
