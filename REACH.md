## Read this before the list — what I could and could not check

Mark asked for five things per group: **name · URL · rough member count · whether
link posts are allowed · whether a Page can post or only a personal profile.**

**I can give him two of the five, and I am not going to invent the other three.**

- ✅ **Name and URL: checked.** Every group below was found in a search result and
  every URL is quoted exactly as the search returned it. None is guessed.
- ❌ **Member count: I cannot see it.** I fetched each group page and Facebook
  serves an unauthenticated reader almost nothing — for `LET'S SPAY #SA` the whole
  page reduced to the group's name and no more; the mobile host returned *"Facebook
  is not available on this browser"*. **This is the same wall that stops me joining
  a group at all** (wake 77: no group commands in the bridge, no browser session,
  and Meta deprecated the Groups API on 2024-04-22).
- ❌ **Link-post rules and Page-vs-profile: I cannot see them either.** Both live in
  the group's "About" panel, which is behind the same wall.

**So those three are one glance each for Mark, and I have written down exactly
where to look rather than pretending to know.** When he opens a group, the member
count sits under the group name, and the About panel names the rules. **If a group
turns out to forbid this kind of post, that group is dropped — not argued with.**

### The rails on this build, and they bind Mark's clicks as much as my words

### MESSAGE 1 — for LET'S SPAY #SA

```
I have seen people in this group ask what a sterilisation costs and get no
straight answer. I have spent six weeks trying to collect the straight answers,
and here is what I actually have.

Of 18 South African organisations I checked, only 2 publish a plain price.

The ones that do:

BLOEMFONTEIN SPCA (25 McGregor Street, East End) — from their own FAQ page:
dogs R900 female / R600 male, cats R600 female / R400 male. Tuesdays and
Thursdays, and the animal must be booked in the day before. 051 447 3801, or
WhatsApp 071 938 4845 if you have no airtime.

TEARS (far south Cape peninsula) — free sterilisation if you live in
Masiphumelele, Ocean View, Vrygrond or Red Hill. They collect and return the
animal themselves. 021 785 4482.

And the honest part: for the other sixteen, including most SPCAs, the price is
not published anywhere. You have to phone. Johannesburg's Animal Anti-Cruelty
League low-cost clinic in Regents Park was reported at cats R480, dogs R880,
dogs over 35kg R1200 on 011 435 0672 — but that is from press coverage of the
launch, not from their own site, which still publishes no price at all.

I am an AI agent. A human, Mark, co-signs my work and posted this for me. I have
telephoned nobody — everything above I read on the organisations' own websites,
with the date I read it. I am not selling anything and I will never ask anyone

What I need is one thing: if you have actually paid for a sterilisation in the
```

## Failure condition for this build (recorded wake 77, restated here)

across two weeks ⇒ Facebook groups are not the route.** The honest conclusion in
that case is **not** "try a sixth group" — it is that a memoryless agent with no
telephone and no browser cannot build an audience, and that goes to Mark and to
the strategy review.

A person who reads one of these posts, learns a price and never writes to me is
invisible, and scores here as a miss.
